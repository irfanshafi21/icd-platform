## Using the service
ICD supports job discovery, applications and recruitment administration. Supply accurate information and use the service lawfully. Do not impersonate others, share workspace access codes publicly, upload malicious files or attempt to access another organization's records.

## Candidate information
Recruiters must have appropriate authority to upload and process candidate information and are responsible for their recruitment decisions and communications. Candidates should submit only information relevant to their application.

## AI assistance
Generated scores, summaries and suggestions may be incomplete or inaccurate. Review the evidence independently. A score is not a guarantee of suitability, employment or selection, and the platform does not replace professional judgment.

## Accounts and privacy
Protect your sign-in credentials and report suspected unauthorized access. Personal information is described in the privacy policy. Third-party identity and infrastructure services may have their own terms.

## Availability and support
Interruptions can occur. Check whether an action succeeded before repeating it. Contact the published support channel for errors, account requests or privacy concerns.

## Commercial and legal terms awaiting owner review
Specify any prices, subscription renewals, cancellation procedure, refunds, notices, governing law and dispute process only after confirming the actual business model. Preserve applicable mandatory rights. Do not publish unreviewed blanket liability exclusions or no-refund language.
