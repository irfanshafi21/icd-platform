YOUR INFORMATION · EXPLAINED

Privacy policy

Effective date: 12 September 2026

This policy explains how ICD Platform (Intelligent Candidate Discovery Platform, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use our website, candidate portal and recruiter workspaces at icd-platform.onrender.com.

Questions or privacy requests? Contact the platform operator at irfanshafi210608@gmail.com. For information an employer holds about your application, also contact that employer directly.

1. Information we process

  • Account information: your email address, account identifier and authentication/session information. When you choose Google sign-in, Google may provide basic profile information such as your name and profile picture.
  • Candidate information: résumés and their extracted text, contact details, education, work history, skills, profile information and applications you submit, or that a recruiting organization uploads.
  • Recruitment records: job requirements, screening scores and explanations, candidate rankings, recruiter notes, application status, interview details and scores, reports and offer-related information.
  • Organization information: company name, logo, website, industry, contact details, registration information, workspace settings and approval requests.
  • Feature and technical data: AI questions and responses, service requests, browser/session information and operational logs that may include IP addresses, timestamps and errors.

Please do not include unnecessary sensitive information, identity documents, passwords or financial details in résumés or AI prompts. Recruiters are responsible for having authority to upload and process candidate information.

2. How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, operate company workspaces, accept applications, parse and screen résumés against job criteria, display recruitment progress, prepare interview questions, coordinate interviews, create reports and offers, deliver service messages, investigate errors and protect the service from misuse.

3. Google sign-in

Google sign-in is handled through Supabase Authentication. We use the identity information returned by Google to create or authenticate your account and display relevant account details. ICD Platform does not receive your Google password. Google sign-in itself does not grant us access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files or calendar.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable. Google sign-in data is not used by ICD Platform for advertising, sold to data brokers, or used to train generalized AI models. You can revoke the Google connection through your Google Account connections; revoking access does not automatically delete information already stored in ICD Platform.

4. AI screening and automated results

When AI features are used, résumé text, job criteria, candidate evidence and relevant prompt content may be sent to configured AI providers. The application supports Groq, Cerebras and Google Gemini, including fallback between configured providers. Provider processing is subject to the applicable provider terms and privacy practices.

The platform calculates scores and rankings and can automatically update interview or offer eligibility using configured thresholds. These outputs can be incomplete or wrong; they are not a guarantee of suitability or employment. Recruiting organizations are responsible for reviewing the evidence and their hiring decisions. To question or correct an assessment, contact the recruiting organization or the platform contact above.

5. Who can receive information

  • Recruiting organizations: the organization receiving your application or processing your résumé can access its recruitment records and export reports. Its own privacy practices govern copies it keeps outside this service.
  • Service providers: Supabase provides authentication and data services; Render hosts the application. Configured AI, email and interview-integration providers receive information needed for the features being used. Email delivery can include report or offer attachments; interview integrations can receive scheduling details.
  • Platform administration: authorized administration and support may process information as needed to operate the service, handle requests and investigate issues.
  • Legal and safety purposes: information may be disclosed when required by applicable law or necessary to address fraud, security incidents or legal claims.

ICD Platform does not sell personal information or use candidate information for targeted advertising. Public job postings and company branding are visible to website visitors; private candidate records are not intended to be public.

6. Storage, retention and deletion

Information is retained to operate accounts and recruitment workflows, maintain relevant records, resolve disputes and meet applicable obligations. Retention varies by record and organization; the app does not apply one automatic deletion period to all recruitment data.

Removing or clearing a saved candidate from a workspace can hide the record without permanently erasing it. For a deletion request, email the contact above with the account email and relevant company or application. Do not send passwords or access codes. We may need to verify your identity and coordinate with the recruiting organization. Records required for legal or security purposes and provider backups may remain subject to their applicable retention processes. We cannot erase reports already downloaded by an employer; request deletion of those copies from the employer.

7. Cookies and your device

We use cookies for authentication and secure sign-in flows. Browser local storage keeps features such as bookmarks, notification read status and AI chat history. Signing out does not necessarily clear all local storage. On a shared device, sign out and clear site data when finished; doing so clears browser-held information, not server records.

8. Security and international processing

The application uses HTTPS, server-managed authentication cookies and company-scoped access controls. No service can guarantee absolute security. Providers may process information in countries other than your own, depending on their infrastructure and service configuration.

9. Your choices and requests

You can choose whether to use Google sign-in, edit profile information where available and contact us to request access, correction or deletion. Depending on your location, applicable law may also provide rights to object to or restrict processing, obtain a copy, withdraw consent or complain to a data-protection authority. We assess requests under applicable law and the relevant organization's responsibilities. Withdrawing access or deleting required information may prevent some features from working.

10. Children and changes to this policy

This service is intended for recruitment and professional use, not for children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has submitted personal information. We may update this policy as the service changes and will revise the effective date. Material new uses requiring consent will be disclosed before that processing begins.
