How can we help?

Practical answers for candidates and hiring teams.

How do I apply for a role?

Sign in to the candidate portal, choose a role and upload a readable PDF or DOCX résumé. Check My applications after submitting to confirm it was received.

Where are interview links and offers?

Open My applications for recorded status updates, interview details and any offer shared to your portal. If information is missing, contact the hiring company. A notification is not a guarantee of selection.

Why was my document skipped?

A résumé already screened for the same job is skipped to preserve its original score. A different job may be screened. Unreadable or non-résumé documents are rejected as files, not as a hiring decision. Temporary processing errors can be retried.

Can I rely only on an AI score?

No. Scores are decision-support estimates, not verified facts or hiring decisions. Recruiters should review the résumé evidence, check inaccuracies and make an independent, fair decision.

What should I do if submission fails?

Check your connection and the recorded result before retrying. Do not repeatedly submit applications, interview invitations or offer emails: an interrupted response does not prove the action failed. No offline submissions are queued.

How do I request correction or deletion?

Use the contact in our privacy policy. Include your account email and relevant application or organization, never passwords or access codes. Clearing saved candidates can hide records without permanently erasing them.

Cookies & device storage

Sign-in cookies support authentication and session recovery. Bookmarks and notification read preferences stay session-only unless you opt into remembering them on this browser. AI conversations are not saved to browser local storage.

Use Device preferences on the home page to change this choice or clear saved preferences. Browser site-data settings can also remove sign-in cookies. These actions do not delete server records or employer-downloaded files.

We have not added advertising analytics, session replay, camera, location or browser push access. In-app notifications do not require push permission.

Installed Python dependency notices (JSON). Bundled visual assets remain subject to separate licence review.

Contact support

Email ICD support. Describe the page, approximate time and error message. Redact personal details in screenshots and never share access codes, résumés or passwords unnecessarily.

For a job's selection decision, interview schedule or offer terms, contact the hiring organization directly.
